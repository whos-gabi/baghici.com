Privacy Policy

Magnetron.io · Effective 17 September 2026 · Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version. Magnetron.io has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects data through those ads. We never receive, store or sell your personal data.

1. Scope and who is responsible

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in the mobile game Magnetron.io (package identifier com.baghici.magnetio), published for Android on Google Play and for iOS on the Apple App Store. It applies to that app only, and not to any other website or product.

2. Data the game itself collects

None that leaves your device. Magnetron.io has no user accounts, no sign-in, no registration, and no backend server operated by us. It contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of our own.

The game writes a single save file into its own private application storage. That file contains:

  • a display name and avatar that you choose inside the game;
  • in-game currency balances and the cosmetic items you own;
  • level progress, statistics for your own runs, and settings such as sound and music volume.

This file is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not backed up to any server we control. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

3. Data collected through advertising

Magnetron.io is free to play and is funded by advertising delivered through Google AdMob, a service provided in the European Economic Area by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Advertising appears as optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, and as occasional full-screen ads shown between game sessions.

To select, deliver, measure and secure those ads, Google and the advertising partners it works with may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers — the Android Advertising ID (AAID), the App Set ID, or on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) where you have permitted it;
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address. The game does not request or use precise GPS location at any time;
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system and version, language, network type, screen characteristics and app version;
  • App activity — ad impressions, views, clicks, rewarded-ad completions, app launches and session events surrounding an ad;
  • Diagnostics — crash logs, latency and technical error reports produced by the advertising library.

In the vocabulary used by the app stores' own disclosure forms, these correspond to Device or other IDs, Location (approximate), App activity and Diagnostics. Each of those categories is both collected and shared with Google, for the purposes of advertising, measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention.

This processing is performed by Google as an independent controller, not by us. We never receive this data in a form that identifies you; the only information available to us is aggregated, non-identifying reporting on impressions, revenue and performance.

4. Third parties

The only third-party service integrated into Magnetron.io is Google AdMob, together with the Google User Messaging Platform used to gather your consent. Google may in turn work with third-party advertising partners who also process the data described above.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • The current list of advertising technology providers Google may work with is published at support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903 and is presented to you inside the consent form described in section 6.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own independent marketing purposes.

6. Consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game presents a consent form supplied by Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified Consent Management Platform, before personalised advertising is shown. The form lists the advertising partners involved and lets you accept or refuse.

You can review or withdraw your choice at any time from the Privacy options entry in the game's Settings screen. Withdrawing consent does not remove advertising: the game continues to show non-personalised ads, which are less targeted but still fund the game. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew it.

7. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On iOS, the operating system asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline, neither we nor our advertising partners will access the IDFA, and you will receive non-personalised ads. Declining does not limit any feature of the game. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

8. Your choices about advertising

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • In the game: the Privacy options entry in Settings, where available in your region.

9. Children

Magnetron.io is not directed at children, is not listed in any store category for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The age rating assigned to the game is shown on each store listing. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact office@baghici.com and we will act on it without undue delay.

10. International transfers

We do not transfer personal data ourselves, because we do not hold any. Google may process and transfer data outside the European Economic Area, including to the United States, under the transfer safeguards described in its own privacy policy, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. How data is kept secure

Your save file is held in the app's private storage, which the operating system isolates from other applications. All network traffic the game generates — which is advertising traffic only — is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS by the Google Mobile Ads library.

We operate no servers and maintain no database, so there is no store of user data on our side that could be breached. Access to the developer and advertising accounts used to publish the game is protected by two-factor authentication.

12. Retention and deletion

We retain no personal data about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. Specifically:

  • Your save file stays on your device until you remove it. To delete it, uninstall the game, or on Android use Settings → Apps → Magnetron.io → Storage → Clear data.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention schedules, described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset the identifier that links it to your device at any time using the controls in section 8.

Because the game has no accounts, there is no account for us to delete. If you would like confirmation of this in writing, write to office@baghici.com.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no data that identifies you, requests concerning advertising data are most effectively directed to Google as the controller of that data. We will assist where we can — write to office@baghici.com and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), dataprotection.ro. If you are elsewhere in the EEA or in the UK, you may complain to your local authority.

14. Residents of US states with privacy laws

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising in exchange for money. Advertising identifiers may nonetheless be treated as "sharing" under some state laws; you can opt out using the device-level controls in section 8, which we honour. You may also contact office@baghici.com to exercise any right available to you under your state's law, and we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address and the "last updated" date above will change. Where a change materially affects how your data is used, it will also be surfaced inside the game, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.

16. Contact

For any question about this policy or about privacy in Magnetron.io, write to office@baghici.com. Postal contact details are available on request and are published on the app store listings where the law requires it.
